# One Image: Friday 20 November 2026

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-blue)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120)
[![Python 3.13](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.13-blue)](https://www.python.org/)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

Entry for the make.fun competition
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120):
sum up 20 November 2026's world news in one image.

The image is a newspaper front page for Friday 20 November 2026.
Its four panels cover the day's biggest stories: the UN's top court
rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Cyclone Akvan
makes landfall on India's east coast with about 300,000 people moved
to shelters; ballistic missiles strike Kyiv in the early hours; and
Anthropic's record IPO closes up 17% on its first day of trading.
The footer strip carries the rest of the day: a Houthi missile hitting
a Riyadh tower, a tanker on fire after a mine strike near the Strait
of Hormuz, Genoa airport halting flights over drones, the US Senate
rejecting an Iran war-powers resolution, Trump's pardon of Pete Rose,
and European allies saying they are ready for talks with Russia.

`draw_entry.py` draws the image from scratch with Pillow
(`entry.png`, JPEG bytes). `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library
only) copies `entry.png` into the output directory as `entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
